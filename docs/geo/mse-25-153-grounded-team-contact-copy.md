# MSE-25.153 — GEO grounded team & contact copy V1

## Goal

Keep the visible team and contact copy aligned with facts that the mini-site can actually demonstrate.

## Changes

- team default copy now describes advisers who are actually presented on the page and the information published about their role and background;
- contact copy describes public agency coordinates and configured local coverage;
- navigation copy refers to services, destinations and inspirations as published content rather than recommendations;
- the review CTA no longer claims that a review helps other travellers choose the agency.

## Guardrails

No inference of adviser expertise, suitability, recommendation, customer familiarity or commercial authority is introduced. Existing `Person`/`TravelAgency` semantics from MSE-25.152 remain unchanged.

## Scope

Mini-site GEO only. No AI agent, Orchestra, Knowledge/remediation or Google review synchronization changes.
