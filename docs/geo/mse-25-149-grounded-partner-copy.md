# MSE-25.149 — GEO grounded partner copy V1

## Goal

Keep visible partner copy aligned with what the public catalogue can actually prove.

## Changes

Removed or replaced copy that implied trust, habitual use or project-specific suitability without an explicit source, including:

- “partenaires de confiance”;
- “mobilisons régulièrement”;
- “spécialistes adaptés à votre projet”;
- “spécialistes complémentaires de votre agence”.

The public copy now uses factual language such as:

- partenaires référencés/publiés;
- sélection complémentaire configurée;
- informations publiées;
- partenaires mis en avant sur ce mini-site.

Partner-specific tags and detail panels remain attributed to the partner itself, not to the local agency.

## Scope

Visible mini-site GEO copy only. No AI-agent, OpenAI Realtime, Orchestra, pricing, availability, stock, booking authority or `knowsAbout` changes.
